# Phase 5: all-in-skeleton outcome of the component-return candidate

Let `B` be the **complete** literal window-history post-[144]
ledger on `G=selected.object`, with one source-and-envelope
`typeBHandoff` witness. Add only the candidate's fifth outcome
`O_F: D_∂=D_I=∅`, where `D=D_F⊔D_I⊔D_∂` is the
assignment-indexed non-centre incidence partition for that same
envelope.

**Exact conditional consequence.** On `B∧O_F`, for every
`(h,a)∈A`, the selected graph has `d_G(a)=3`, and `F`
contains all three edges incident to `a`: the assigned centre
edge `ha` and both non-`h` edges from `D_F`. Thus
`d_F(a)=3`. There is no assigned-neighbour exterior edge to
start the component-return construction, and no extra
`I=E(G[S])∖E(F)` edge represented in `D`. This is a
genuine description of the selected outcome, but the outcome
predicate itself already says exactly where those two incidences
lie; it does not pay an original pair or shrink the parent
residual by a new inference.

**Published B1 consumer check.** The paper's local B1 charge
applies to a *marked* Type B fan-window profile with
certificate data, a count `c` of cubic-closed neighbours of
the entire centre, and a positive-deficit condition
`D_B=c-(11-k)/4>0` when a deficit is to be paid. The
handoff retains an `Envelope` with a nonempty assigned
`K_h` and a pairwise `FanSafe` relation, plus packing
and core. It does not provide the certificate labelling
`S_h` on all of `N(h)`, the fan-window incidence profile,
an identification of this envelope with a B1 marked fan,
`k≤8`, or a positive `D_B`. Even `K_h` can have only
one member. The proved `d_F(a)=3` for assigned `a`
cannot be substituted for those inputs or for `c` on all
neighbours. The component-return candidate has no B1
conditional payoff on `O_F`.

**Other closure currencies.** No accepted-length cycle follows
from `d_F(a)=3`: the extra two edges may form a tree-like
part of `F`, and the existing `selection` already forbids
an accepted cycle in `G`. No smaller admissible graph,
target-response transport, or strict progress order was
constructed. The exact surplus and original blocked-pair
accounts have no new debit from `O_F`. Therefore
`B∧O_F→False`, a positive physical cost, and a
branch-reducing replacement are all **unproved** by this
candidate.

**Gate decision.** Reject the proposed component-return move
as a productive move: the all-in-`F` outcome has no
significant conditional payoff beyond its own incidence
definition. Keep the full `B∧O_F` residual open; do not
route it to a homogeneous cap, B1 discharge, or near-cubic
spine. This rejects this particular move, not the possibility
of a different argument on the same complete residual. The
first missing inference for a B1-based attempt is an actual
certificate-marked fan-window profile tied to this envelope
with its degree/deficit and incidence data. The next
structural selection must either derive that interface or
use a mechanism with a verified payoff on `O_F`.
