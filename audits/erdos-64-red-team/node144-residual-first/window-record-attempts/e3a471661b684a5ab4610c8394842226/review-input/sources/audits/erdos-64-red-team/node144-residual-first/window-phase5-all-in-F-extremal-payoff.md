# [144a] window history: conditional payoff on the saturated two-tail outcome

Let `B` be the complete literal window-history [144a] ancestry on `G`, with one fixed matching/star source pattern and its two distinct equal-routing-label selected edges. Keep their chosen demands `d_L,d_R`, common root `ρ`, pair-specific vertex supports `S_L,S_R`, and endpoint buffers. Among **all** pairs of valid `RoutingConfiguration` paths with these same fixed data, choose `(r_L,r_R)` maximizing `m=commonPrefixLength(r_L,r_R)`. Such a pair exists: the published configurations supply one pair, every simple route has at most `|V(G)|` vertices, and hence the nonempty set of possible `m` has a maximum. The claim below is conditional on the pair having a first separator `h`; the parallel case is handled separately.

Suppose its source-bound first-separator envelope has `D_I=D_∂=∅`. The reviewed exact two-tail topology gives distinct assigned neighbours `a,b`, with `r_L` leaving `h` through `a` and `r_R` through `b`, and gives `a` as an **internal** vertex of the trimmed right tail. In particular `a` occurs exactly once on `r_R` after `h` and before its original terminal `v_R`. Let `C` be the common initial list from `ρ` through `h` (length `m`), and let `Q` be the original right-route suffix beginning at that occurrence of `a` and ending at `v_R`. Set

```
r'_R = C ++ Q.
```

The last vertex of `C` is `h`, the first of `Q` is `a`, and `ha` is an actual graph edge from the original left route. Thus the concatenation is a graph chain. Since `r_R` is simple, its original prefix through `h` and its later suffix `Q` are disjoint; `Q` itself is simple. Hence `r'_R` is simple. It has the original root `ρ` and terminal `v_R`, so its `issued` token-root and `lands` right-local-buffer fields are unchanged. Every vertex of `C` and `Q` already occurs on `r_R`, so `r'_R` lies inside the *right* support `S_R`; this uses the literal `RoutingConfiguration.inside` field, which is a vertex-membership condition. No assumption that edge `ha` lay on an old right route is needed.

The unchanged `r_L` and new `r'_R` agree on the original `m` common vertices **and** on the next vertex `a`. Their common-prefix length is at least `m+1`, contradicting the choice of `m` as maximum over valid routes for these fixed source demands. Consequently

```
B ∧ MaxPrefix(r_L,r_R) ∧ FirstSeparator(h,a,b)
  ∧ SameSourceEnvelope(h,a,b) ∧ D_I=∅ ∧ D_∂=∅ → False.
```

This is a local conditional exclusion of the *all-in-`F` envelope generated by the extremal routes*. It neither says that every possible handoff envelope has an escape nor that the original pattern is paid. Equal source terminals, a disconnected or adjacent core, shared route segments, and zero-length arms do not create exceptions: the earlier topology task proved all-in-`F` forces both arms positive and `a` internal to the right tail. The argument does not infer a target cycle or a quotient. It has mathematical review status only until an owner-local Lean fact is implemented and kernel checked.
