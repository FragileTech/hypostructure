# Roadmap to a Binary, Proof-Carrying Regularity/Singularity Engine

## 1. Non-negotiable objective

The final engine must have **exactly two semantic outputs**:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Engine}(\mathfrak P,u_0)
\in
\left\{
\operatorname{Regular}(C_{\mathrm{reg}}),
\operatorname{Singular}(u_\ast,\mathfrak a,C_{\mathrm{sing}})
\right\}.
}
\]

Here:

- \(C_{\mathrm{reg}}\) is a checkable proof that no ordinary maximal branch generated by the public presentation reaches the complete continuation-failure target.
- \(u_\ast\) is an independently generated ordinary public PDE orbit.
- \(\mathfrak a\) is its complete joint-saturated structural atom.
- \(C_{\mathrm{sing}}\) verifies the weak equation, initial and boundary data, physical ledger, all auxiliary graph relations, independent origin, and cofinal target contact.

There is no `Open`, `Unknown`, `Uncontrolled source`, `Unclassified remainder`, or `Stationary model found` terminal.

A successor, equality kernel, positive functional, compactified point, recurrent law, graph-boundary value, zero-price state, or prospective atom is an **internal compiler state**. It must be processed again.

An implementation that has not finished may still have a live queue, but that
queue is not a mathematical output. A deployable decision engine therefore
requires a separate finite-progress theorem. In this roadmap that theorem is
the generated Noetherian successor criterion; target-aligned accountability
alone does not provide it.

## 2. The exact gap already identified by the manuscript

Your manuscript already proves finite exact-support accounting: for \(N\) normalized crossings,

\[
N=\sum_{\varnothing\ne A\subseteq\Channels}S_{A,N},
\]

so at least one exact support accumulates an unbounded amount of normalized target progress. But the manuscript then correctly states that this does **not yet** prove

\[
\sup_N S_{A,N}=+\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sup_N P_{A,N}=+\infty
\quad\text{or an ordinary source-owned terminal orbit}.
\]

It explicitly lists the missing pieces:

1. identification of the accounting increment with the local source-current amplitude;
2. a source-conversion identity whose storage and equality successors retain the complete source word;
3. termination or ordinary realization of every cofinal re-entry chain; and
4. compatibility of that realization with the public continuation graph.

That is the correct spine of the roadmap.

Your later binary contact-cell theorem,

\[
\Reach_\Sigma^{\Phys}=0
\iff
\mathscr C_{A,\Sigma}=\varnothing
\quad\text{for all }A,
\]

is presently a **semantic specification** of what regularity means. It becomes an actionable engine theorem only after the roadmap below proves that contact-cell emptiness or nonemptiness is actually certified by the generated proof-or-realization process.

The distinction is:

\[
\textbf{Accounting totality}
\neq
\textbf{terminal completeness}
\neq
\textbf{effective halting}.
\]

You have most of the first. The roadmap supplies the second and third.
