# Audit structural exhaustion

Read `tools/methodology_gate/README.md`, `policy/workflow.json`, and the exact
branch record. For a new taskflow run, distinguish three statuses: accepted
atomic task, certified productive move, and closed branch. A task's cited
artifact and two distinct review records are evidence of review, not automatic
mathematical truth. Inspect the actual inference at its stated domain. For an
existing isolated stage-runner record, preserve its accepted stage history and
use its original validator and review contract.

1. Reconstruct Phase 0: source revision, unchanged endpoint, tagged producer,
   complete inherited conjunction, actual object identities and domains,
   minimality, imports, accounts, and open outcomes. Do not combine premises
   from different incoming alternatives.
2. Inspect prior-use rows per object and result. Logical facts may participate
   in new interactions; finite resource reuse needs a balance or disjointness
   proof. Check transport after representation changes.
3. Inspect each interaction register entry for a real mathematical relation
   among its ingredients, including higher-order conjunctions. An observable
   alone does not establish a useful value of it.
4. Check the selected structural tension preceded technique selection and
   has a supported prospective effect on the actual residual. Catalogue moves
   with exact prerequisites and all exceptional outcomes.
5. For every authorized outcome, inspect the conditional implication from the
   full branch to a closure or precise local payoff. A correct irrelevant lemma,
   notation change, bare split, or unbounded finite table is intermediate work.
6. Match every constructed witness, preservation proof, account update, and
   coverage argument to the actual objects. Expose the first unsupported
   inference and keep the successful prefix.
7. Confirm that new facts were combined with retained restrictions before
   unrelated exploration, affected accounts were reconciled, and newly enabled
   old moves revisited. Test constraint, compression, and quantity separately.
8. Keep every survivor open with its full ancestry and next local obligation.
   A closed sibling does not close the parent. Check AND-case coverage,
   recursive decrease if used, exact endpoint composition, required kernel
   checks, and synchronized manuscript/diagram views before claiming closure.

Stale evidence invalidates its dependent tasks and move credit. Failed search
never selects a negative mathematical arm. If open leaves remain, report the
exact reduction boundary. Do not claim that workflow metadata or a long task
list proves a theorem.
