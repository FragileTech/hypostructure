# Residual-first structural execution

Read `tools/methodology_gate/README.md`, `policy/workflow.json`, and the generated
`policy/references/task-prompt.md`. Use `tools/methodology_gate/taskflow.py` for
new runs. Existing stage-runner records may continue through their locked
reviewed prefix; do not silently translate accepted stages into new proof facts.

Start with Phase 0: pin source revision, exact endpoint, tagged incoming arm,
actual objects and domains, all inherited premises, minimality, imports, and
quantitative accounts. A merged branch never receives the union of its arms'
premises. Preserve the full residual throughout. Do not replace the local
claim by an unconditioned theorem on a larger class.

Create one task contract per inference, construction, comparison, or check.
Use the phase/task kinds and required fields in `policy/workflow.json`. Each
worker receives the pinned branch and accepted inputs, proves its one requested
output, and exposes the first missing inference as smaller obligations. Side
observations go into the research record without changing the active task.
Only cited evidence accepted by two distinct reviewers completes a task.
A proposed statement, a reviewed mathematical statement, and a kernel-checked
implementation have different evidence status.

Follow Phases 1–8 in order of dependency, not as one prompt per phase:

1. Record prior structural uses per object and conclusion, including remaining
   quantitative capacity. A logical bound can be reused for a new interaction.
2. Inventory actual observables and their unresolved interactions, including
   higher-order conjunctions. No technique selection or invented values.
3. Select a supported structural tension and compare a serious rival before
   choosing a mechanism.
4. Catalogue exact textbook moves, prerequisites, exception arms, and attempt
   fingerprints. Missing premises remain obligations.
5. Establish `B ∧ O → G` for every proposed outcome on the full branch. `G`
   must be exact closure or a productive local restriction with a specified
   continuation. Finish prerequisite submoves before dependent construction.
6. Execute one authorized construction obligation at a time on the retained
   objects. Prove actual witnesses, preservation, accounts, and coverage.
7. Integrate every new fact with retained restrictions and revisit enabled
   moves. Test constraint, compression, and quantity separately. Retain each
   survivor with its full ancestry and next local obligation.
8. Verify the exact endpoint, all AND-case branches, transport or recursive
   decrease, required kernel checks, and synchronized proof views.

A completed task is not automatically a productive move. A productive move
requires accepted local payoff on every outcome. A branch closes only when
all reachable outcomes compose to its exact endpoint. A bound on one marked
family does not bound the whole graph. Finite enumeration keeps all surviving
rows open. A failed search never proves a negative case.

Repair the first defective decision and invalidate only its dependents. Keep
unaffected accepted facts and closed siblings. An empty ready queue diagnoses
missing work; it is not closure. For EG formalization, preserve the canonical
`ExactLedger`, `FactInputs`, `FactManifest`, and sealed owner-local executor.
Workflow metadata cannot transport proof facts around those APIs.
