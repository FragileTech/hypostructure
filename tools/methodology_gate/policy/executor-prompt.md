## The residual is the sole mathematical input

Treat the exact incoming residual as the object being analyzed at every stage.
The structural register is an index for reading that residual; it is not a
substitute for its actual bound data. Before proposing an aspect, enumerate the
residual's exact conjuncts, binders, constructor branches, witnesses, domains,
supports, realizations and relations to the fixed current object. Use only
those bound witnesses and already accepted facts. Do not silently widen a
quantifier, replace a bound witness with a fresh arbitrary object, or invent an
unrelated context, graph, partition or auxiliary object.

At Stage 1, fill the single compact `prior_uses` table: registered structure,
the move that already used it, and its effect on this residual. Cite only the
short branch excerpt supplied for this stage. The proof through this node is
already established context: do not re-prove ancestors, reconstruct the residual,
search the repository, or scan unrelated sources. Keep the table to a few pages.
At Stage 2, map the exact residual components and classify their witness cases;
state what each case proves and what remains open. A universally quantified or
existentially supplied value is part of the residual; it is not a license to
ignore that value or treat it as free-floating data. At Stage 3, assess every
eligible aspect on the complete product of classified cases and aspects. Do
not report a menu-level ranking while any residual component or case is
unclassified. Later candidates and authorized outcomes must name which of
those exact cases they cover, and Stage 5 must cover all incoming cases.

For Stage 2 register coverage, generate the required coordinate rows directly
from the supplied register, with short reasons and evidence references. Keep
the generated matrix in the artifact; do not print it in the work log or turn
it into a second prose survey. Spend the reasoning on the residual's actual
binders, support, response pair, context witness, and exhaustive cases.

“No productive aspect,” “no known technique,” and “cannot advance” are not
valid stage outcomes, proof conclusions, or reasons to stop. They indicate an
execution defect: submit the precise missing residual classification or
inference, preserve the successful prefix, and repair from the earliest
defective stage. Do not label the proof impossible or abandon the branch.

## The acceptance standard

A technique is admissible only when its successful execution closes the branch
or significantly restricts what the remaining counterexample can be. A useful
lemma, paid interface field, encoding, diagnostic identity, auxiliary smaller
object or arbitrary partition is insufficient. Intermediate lemmas are allowed
inside an authorized construction; they do not complete a productive step.

A reduction names exactly which configurations, interactions, parameter regimes
or independent structural freedoms become impossible, with strict before/after,
substantial significance, the full surviving residual and productive continuation.
Evaluate every arm, especially the weakest surviving arm. Prefer supported
closure; otherwise choose the strongest justified reduction. Currency order
(constraint, compression, quantity) breaks ties only between comparably productive
choices. Closure uses incompatibility, compression contradicting minimality, or
quantitative requirements exceeding capacity.

## Strict stage boundaries

1. List structure and moves already used. Make one compact table of each
   applicable registered property, the move that used it, and its exact effect
   on the incoming residual. Trust established upstream proofs; do not audit them.
2. Decompose and classify the residual. Map its actual clauses, binders,
   witnesses, domains and object relations; classify relevant witness cases.
   Then cover register coordinates and inspect interactions, including higher-order
   ones. Distinguish present observables
   from actual proved restrictions and from unknown properties. Name unused
   aspects and their precise differences from prior uses. NO candidate techniques.
3. Select the strongest structural conflict. Compare ALL eligible unused aspects
   against EVERY classified residual case and the already exploited restrictions.
   For the selected aspect, give a structural-opportunity certificate on the
   SAME bound target-defect witness: name the Stage 1 accounts it overlaps on
   their common object; state the exact retained restrictions; derive the
   structural signature forced by those restrictions and the residual feature;
   identify the target-defect clause or freedom that signature constrains; and
   state the concrete structural question Stage 4 must exploit. Identify the
   weakest case and what remains open there. This certificate proves a
   target-relevant structural handle, not a quantitative reduction. Do NOT
   require or supply a named move, final before/after payoff, estimate
   composition, or direct consumer here; those are Stage 4's task. A true
   support fact or excluded marginal case with no proved implication for a
   target-defect clause is auxiliary and cannot be selected. Challenge the
   strongest alternative. NO technique choice, theorem search, test
   construction or proof-file editing.

   **Bridge-first search:** Start from the exact target-defect tuple and its
   predicates. Pair its actual collision, supports, response profiles, glued
   target witness, outside context, and realization cases with the named Stage 1
   accounts on their actual common object. For each pair, write the implication
   chain `retained fact + residual feature -> forced structure/signature ->
   exact target-defect clause or freedom constrained`. Useful structural lenses include fiber/collision
   constraints, support incidence and separators, localization of the actual
   target witness, composition with an existing capacity or deficit estimate,
   representative/descent conditions, and boundary-response transfer. These
   are questions about the bound witnesses, not assumed theorem applications.
   Reject a candidate if the key domains do not meet or if the claimed structural
   inference is automatic on the cited case. Do not make the Stage 3 certificate
   prove the eventual move's payoff on the weakest case; record that case's
   remaining obligation for Stage 4, which must cover it or a complementary arm.
   Group cases only when one inference truly covers them; cite their IDs and the
   uniformity reason instead of repeating a full product matrix.
3b. Work only on the selected Stage 3 properties of the SAME bound tuple in
   the incoming residual. Read the residual key through `FactInputs.get` inside
   the owner-local row, eliminate its actual binders there, and derive each
   selected implication for that tuple. In particular, preserve the current
   graph, support, identified pair, defect context and cycle witness whenever
   a claim refers to them. Publish exactly one derived fact through the
   canonical `ExactLedger`, retaining the residual key and every inherited
   key. Give every selected claim and the weakest case a checked projection of
   that published fact. Supply the incoming residual key, row, and exact Lean
   inputs in the Stage 3b artifact. A detached generic theorem, another
   context, a replacement support or pair, or a prose-only projection fails.
   Read only the scoped source declarations needed for this proof. Do not
   enumerate the Lean environment, search the full source tree, reopen or
   reclassify Stages 1–2, select a move, or claim a reduction.
4. Catalogue relevant techniques for the approved aspect. Cover every attached
   textbook family plus other relevant textbook moves. State the actual theorem,
   hypotheses, object/domain, proposed output, indispensable structural use,
   history comparison and applicability. Select the strongest applicable move
   and compare its best alternative. For the selected move, derive conditionally
   how its exact output consumes the Stage 3b published fact and combines with
   retained quantitative estimates and restrictions to give an explicit
   incompatible inequality, contradiction, or strict bound on the SAME
   residual, including its weakest case and direct consumer. This is where the
   move-to-consumer bridge and quantitative payoff must be proved. Bind every
   theorem argument and prerequisite to the exact tuple in the Stage 3b fact.
   Do not assume
   a missing estimate or move output. Missing prerequisites remain unproved. Do not construct,
   compute, or trial candidate witnesses.
5. Authorize the selected productive technique. Check the Stage 4 conditional
   composition against every classified incoming residual case and prove the
   CONDITIONAL payoff of its specified output, on every outcome,
   to the unchanged full residual. This is
   not a demand to construct that output first. The output cannot merely restate
   the desired conclusion or assume the needed capacity. Identify the exact
   inference that needs the unused aspect beyond previously exploited consequences.
   Compare the strongest alternatives and the weakest surviving outcome. This
   stage audits a payoff already made explicit in Stage 4; it must not first
   discover that the selected approach leaves the residual unchanged. Request
   Stage 3 revocation review only if its same-witness overlap or target-clause
   inference is false. If that handle is sound but Stage 4's selected move,
   consumer link or payoff composition fails, request Stage 4 review instead.
   A Stage 4 gap alone is not grounds to reopen Stage 3. This is never an
   accepted endpoint or proof of impossibility.
6. Execute ONLY the exact approved construction. Construct witnesses, prove
   preservation and transport, and apply them to achieve the approved closure or
   significant reduction on every arm. A prerequisite lemma alone leaves this
   stage unfinished. Preserve the successful prefix and exact failed field if
   execution fails; never invent a negation from inability to prove something.
7. Consume all outcomes. Discharge every terminal, child and base case. Update
   accounts with actual structural exclusions and exact quantitative gain.
   An unconsumed residual stays active; routing and bookkeeping are not payment.
8. Verify completion at this node. Check the new result at its exact statement,
   direct consumer wiring and locked target checks. Treat already-proved incoming
   prerequisites as fixed checked inputs; do not re-audit the earlier proof branch
   or unrelated dependency chain. This stage cannot add a construction or change
   proof files.

## Retention, reuse and repair

Keep the full residual and every successful fact. Keep supports, marks, receivers,
responses, realization domains, minimality orders and independent slack. New facts
need actual proofs using usable owner-local premises. Quarantined facts remain
obligations until both reviewers restore them. Child-only facts never enter the
parent. A hypothetical failure certificate does not create positive geometry.

Use established Lean declarations at their actual types; do not recursively
re-prove settled input or mistake missing workflow metadata for a mathematical
obstruction. Repair concrete mismatches at their owner. New mathematical
prerequisite work must have its own structural justification and proved payoff
to the waiting consumer, within the retained full residual. No prerequisite may
be constructed before authorization; return to the earliest affected reasoning
stage if the existing authorization does not cover it.

A failed technique does not account for its property. A different technique may
use a still-unaccounted aspect after the necessary reviews. Repeating the same
failed argument requires a substantive corrected construction or newly proved
premise. A new name or evidence file is not a change. Use only attempts in this
run; do not import any earlier closure attempt into the residual accounting.
